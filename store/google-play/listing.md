# 24 HR Towing

## Short description

Run a heavy wrecker through a Wisconsin blizzard. Something is moving the cars.

## Full description

Arlene has a brown sedan off the highway. She gives you a road, a landmark and a plate. The rest is your job.

It’s 1996 in Tamarack County, Wisconsin. The tow ban keeps most drivers home. You run Brandt Towing & Recovery, and the sheriff still needs the roads cleared. Find your turn by the dash compass, roadside fire numbers and a paper plat book. If you miss it in the snow, call dispatch for a talk-in.

Park the wrecker, climb down and carry the cable to the car. Set your angle and work the winch at the side panel. Some drivers can take their car from there. Others leave you with a loaded wheel-lift, a longer braking distance and a slip to fax back at the yard.

Diesel, tire chains and a second winch all come out of the till. Take the calls that pay for them. There are stranded motorists, heavy recoveries and sheriff’s holds with paperwork of their own.

On some runs, three lights keep pace out beyond the fence. You can pull over to investigate or finish the tow. Arlene will keep a place for you on the rotation.

Play offline in landscape. Switch between cab and chase views when driving, then work on foot. All conversations appear in subtitles. Your shift saves on your device.
