# 24 HR Towing privacy policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Matt Koski develops 24 HR Towing. The game runs offline. It stores your shift, vehicles, paperwork, story progress and settings on your device. The game does not collect or share personal data, display ads, run analytics or require an account.

Your saved game stays on the device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. On Android you can clear it from Settings under Apps, 24 HR Towing, Storage.

If you email support, Matt receives your email address and the information you choose to send. He uses it to respond to your request. For privacy questions or to request deletion of a support email, contact maccam912@gmail.com.
